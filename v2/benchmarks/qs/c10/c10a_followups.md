# C10a: conditional collection-policy follow-ups

C10a tracks collection choices and one runner repair left open after C10
tranche 1. These are follow-up gates, not a second acceptance of the first tranche.
Each future experiment needs its own frozen sources, controls, finite resources,
matched complete outcomes and fresh confirmation. A loss by one tested arm does
not reject the broader mechanism.

| ID | C10-owned question and executable source | v2 boundary and revisit trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C10a-01 | Discriminant-normalized SIQS and representation-coupled multiplier scoring. Yamaquasi `PolyType`/`select_siqs_factors`/`prepare_a`, JavaMath `SIQS`/`SIQSPolyGenerator`, Prague `CalculateKRoot`/`ComputeC` use related mod-8 choices; CADO `las-norms` illustrates the distinct standard form. | `Polynomial`, `PolynomialFamily`, `select_multiplier`, `prime_power_roots`, relation identities and checkpoints must jointly represent the factor of four and exceptional primes. Start only after a scoped exact-value/root cost and useful-yield case justifies the representation work; coordinate root-kernel interfaces with A1a and residual provenance with C9. Independent oracles exist, but no production normalized arm has been tested. |
| C10a-02 | Actual A-product **population** selection beyond a fixed 16-family permutation, and **separately** inter-family overlap. Yamaquasi `select_a` ranks/truncates a wider exact-product pool and then sorts selected A values numerically; FLINT `qsieve_init_A`/`qsieve_next_A` uses a product window and corrective factor; CUDA-MPQS `init_a_factors` seeds an actual-product window then `advance_a_factors` swaps neighboring primes. Prague `CreateNewAForSiqs` uses quantized log-distance and admits no two-entry diversity check; Alpertron `getFactorsOfA` checks within-A indices. | `AssignmentStream` already has an exact corrective flyer; the tested ordering cannot change its 16-family membership. On 30-digit training it yielded 411,862 more candidates, of which 409,320 more were exact-refinement rejections, and its extra relations did not reduce complete cost. A new bounded pool/window or independent spacing policy requires a measured link between exact product error/overlap and useful post-filter rows/dependencies, with construction, recovery and replay included. The fixed permutation's loss does not reject either broader mechanism. |
| C10a-03 | Bounded per-job threshold feedback. YAFU `siqs_sync` tests neighboring **post-small-prime trial-factoring bit cutoffs** for relation rate; Prague `LineSiever::RunSieve` actively calls `ThresholdOptimizer` to change its sieve byte initialization; CUDA-MPQS optional autotuning uses a different, large-input cost model. These are distinct controllers, not one portable threshold rule. | `SieveCollector._bounds`/`_candidate_passes` already adapt to exact values; `threshold_extra` can lose candidates. Revisit if candidate recovery is a measured limiting cost or an exact held-out rejected-position audit shows useful surplus. Freeze pilot epochs and loss bounds, charge and checkpoint controller state, and obtain C9 coordination for any changed admission boundary. A relation-rate or residual-magnitude improvement alone is insufficient. |
| C10a-04 | Coupled multiplier, eligible base count/value, interval, A-factor count and family pool policy. msieve `choose_multiplier`/`poly_init`, Yamaquasi `select_multiplier`/`FBase::new`, FLINT `qsieve_knuth_schroeppel`, Alpertron `FactoringSIQS` use different coupled heuristics. | `select_multiplier`, `build_factor_base`, `a_target`, `half_width`, `factor_count` and `pool_size` already expose most mechanisms. Revisit when complete cost and exact useful-yield profiles identify a remaining size-band gap after isolated policies. Include even-multiplier gcd/mod-8 branches and actual base endpoint/count; preserve B1/C1 losses. Do not copy native constants or optimize one parameter in isolation. |
| C10a-05 | Fixed collection excess and retry cadence. FLINT `qsieve_factor_with_tune` processes relations then can grow its base; msieve and Alpertron use different base-plus-excess targets. | `QSJob._solve`, `row_excess`, `filter_row_growth` and `weight_two` control when original relations reach filtering and extraction. The fresh 50-digit control already recorded 6,203 filter calls and 6,194 solve calls across nine complete jobs, with 85.020 and 83.518 seconds in those stages, about 11.8% of complete wall time combined. This is a concrete **study trigger**, not evidence that a different cadence wins: C10a must compare usable rows, dependencies, proper factors and total cost. C10a owns fixed scheduling policy; C9 owns yield-dependent target feedback and residual-product rules, while matrix representation, solver and storage remain with A8/B6/B7/C4/D1/D2. |
| C10a-06 | Make repeated-lease yield markers idempotent in `c10.study.measure`. The 50-digit training extension saved all 11 matched groups and expenses, then the second lease hit `FileExistsError` saving an already present `train-yield-50.json`. | Before a later C10 measurement, allow an atomic update or lease-specific marker while retaining prior raw evidence. Test repeated lease and interruption paths under a **new** source identity; do not rewrite this tranche's frozen runner, controls, rows or captured incident. The 50-digit fresh confirmation preserved its first yield marker under a lease-specific name before resuming. |
| C10a-07 | Scoped automatic use of the accepted 2 MiB power plan. The first tranche selected the plan for exact 40- and 50-digit growing-DLP bundles, but left `power_plan_bytes=0` as the general default and did not add a production size-band selector. | C10 owns an explicit, versioned selector for those accepted bundle identities after the C9 integration baseline is settled. Preserve caller overrides and legacy checkpoint identity; prove selected configurations differ from the frozen controls only in the plan allowance, and recheck owned memory, replay, clean-checkout tests and complete factors through the reachable entry point. Do not infer a blanket 40/50-digit `SIQSConfig` default from two measured bundles. E1 owns later combined portfolio/default acceptance, not this C10 selector. |

Existing owner handoffs stay outside C10a: A1a owns presieve, root-transition
tables, derivative-inverse reuse and cross-polynomial sparse-hit kernels (including
CUDA-MPQS's batch mechanism); C8 owns CADO's sorted CRT half-sum scheduler;
C9/C9a/C1a own residual admission, splitting, matching, graph and retention;
F6 owns higher-arity relations; E1 owns combined portfolio acceptance. C10a
may use those measured results as controls but must not reimplement their work.
The C9 literature verification (`19bb25d` on `codex/c9-large-prime-yield`,
`v2/benchmarks/qs/c9/c9_literature_verification.md`) separately tracks
asymmetric large-prime bounds, residual scoring and yield feedback; none of
those are C10a proposals.
