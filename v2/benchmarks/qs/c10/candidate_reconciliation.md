# C10 tranche 1: mechanism reconciliation

This ledger classifies the mechanisms traced in the [ranked research audit](research.md)
and [supplemental caller audit](source_supplement.md) against the
[delivered results](tranche1_results.md). Exact source revisions, file hashes
and licenses are in the [source manifest](../../inputs/controls/c10_source_pins.json).
`Tested` refers to the named isolated arm and regime; it never rejects every
implementation of a broader mechanism. `Equivalent` means the specified v2
mechanism already exists, not that its settings are optimal. `Rejected` applies
only to a stated direct transfer or measured arm. `Handed off` preserves the
existing owner and does not claim a C10 implementation.
