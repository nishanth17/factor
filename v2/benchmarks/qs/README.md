C10a-07 [results](../RESULTS.md#c10a-07-face-and-queue) summarize stopped
diagnostics, superseded screens and final adoption with distinct identities.
Detailed reports and generated captures stay local under `v2/audit/` and
`v2/benchmarks/results/`; earlier published reports remain in pinned history.

Use the [benchmark workflow](../README.md) before running an experiment.
Compare complete costs, useful dependencies and output validation with the
correct source/configuration pin. Prefix throughput, extra rows, faster
refusal and synthetic capacity are diagnostics. [Results](../RESULTS.md)
record observed scope; [STATUS](../../STATUS.md) owns current defaults and
still-deferred confirmation. Historical controls are not silently migrated to
new policies.
