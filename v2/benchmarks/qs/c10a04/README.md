# C10a-04 compact-base calibration

`configuration.py` resolves the exact counted-base bundles, `selection.py`
selects inputs, `accounting.py` enforces finite ledgers, and `study.py` contains
the original frozen multi-band runner. Required controls and certified corpora
remain under [inputs](../../inputs/README.md).

`short_decisions.py` preserves the tested three-input/three-seed schedule and
preliminary early-stop rules from the completed bounded follow-up. Those rules
remain descriptive; they do not establish the ordinary performance gate.

The retired short and 65-digit spike/resume campaigns are available in
[pinned history](https://github.com/nishanth17/factor/tree/23b71fa723b7982f1b1ceb18c426036e5e39290d/v2/studies).
Replay them from their original source revision with unchanged controls;
current code and a historical source freeze are different identities.
Detailed reports stay local. See the [results summary](../../RESULTS.md#c10a-04-larger-band-routing)
for measurements, routing limits and historical replay guidance.
