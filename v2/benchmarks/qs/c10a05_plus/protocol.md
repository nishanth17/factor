# C10a-05 larger-band default-scope extension

This study begins after the accepted 50-digit C10a-05 default was merged at
`3d4bf42`. The request is to consider all inputs of at least 50 digits. The
existing C9 and C10 selectors actually cover exactly 50 and exactly 40/50
digits, respectively. An unbounded digit range cannot be validated by a finite
sample; promote only bands with complete matched evidence. Preserve the closed
C10a-05 protocol, controls, results, code and verdict.

The first revealed, certified balanced inputs at 51/55/60 digits are a
**feasibility screen**, not acceptance samples. Compare current mainline
`SIQSConfig()` against the existing 50-digit C9/C10 collection bundle without
changing its arithmetic. The reference configuration has a smaller memory
allowance and finite family count: this comparison evaluates full default
policy, not an isolated scheduling effect. The revealed 55-digit longer call
uses the currently omitted 1200-second grant; it is neither a universal cap
nor a sufficient completion criterion for all 55-digit inputs. No 60-digit
promotion follows a partial-yield screen.

If feasibility supports 51 digits, use three **revealed training numbers**
(including the 51-digit screen input) and four arms: current reference,
C9 growing DLP without C10's power plan (window pacing), C9+C10 plan with
window pacing, and that plan with collection pacing. These separate the C10
plan and C10a-05 pacing within the C9 bundle. Between candidate arms, retain
polynomial, base, interval, scoring, residual, graph, storage, matrix and
budget fields. Reference versus bundle measures the whole default switch.
Seeds 7/29/47 crossed with three independent products yield nine matched
groups; repeats are not additional independent numbers. Randomize arm order.
Extend to eighteen if within-number paired signs mix, repeated-cell wall
drift exceeds 15%, or early/late three-group mean effects differ by 15 points.
Keep all attempts and reservations after interruption. Preselect by complete
count, then aggregate censored cost, preserving current default on a tie.

Freeze runtime/helpers, protocol, source pins, configurations, inspected
training inputs, seeds, selection/stability rules and caps in committed
controls before accepted timing. Training calls have 600-second wall and CPU
caps; confirmation calls have 720 seconds, each also limited to 10^13 work,
its arm's declared owned storage, and a 3 GiB RSS watchdog. These windows
have headroom over the 174-second complete feasibility call and do not assert
coverage beyond the sampled population. Unresolved outcomes retain actual
time/work and cost their full cap for selection. The aggregate wall/CPU charge
is 36,000 seconds, including a conservative 3,500-second prepaid feasibility,
generation and QA reservation; training and confirmation reserve 20,000 and
12,000 seconds respectively. A whole missing group must fit before it starts;
each arm reserves before execution. Repeated leases never overwrite rows.

Selection and all training receipts/hash identities are committed **before**
generating three independent fresh 51-digit certified products. Exclude all
versioned input numbers; disclose the generator's large-factor-of-p−1
conditioning. Warm each arm in every process lease with at least three seconds
of validated PyPy 3.11 wall and CPU work. Cold starts are separate. Fresh
confirmation repeats the nine/eighteen matched design, reports completion,
proper factors, stage and complete cost, work, stored rows/partials and memory.
Use the frozen 4000-draw hierarchical input-then-observation bootstrap for
complete/capped cost. A new default needs zero correctness failures, stable
planned samples and a complete-factor benefit without declared 40/50-digit
regression. If the current reference never completes, report completion gain
and actual time separately; its fast unsuccessful stop is not a speedup.
No fresh 51-digit result justifies a 52+ or unlimited-range performance claim.

The prior 741-test full suite, lint, legacy checkpoints and source manifests
must keep passing after any default change. Use the shared machine-wide
performance lock and external PyPy implementing Python 3.11. Preserve local
raw receipts in ignored `results/c10a05_plus/`, version required inputs and
study sources, and verify tests/imports from committed files. Mainline has
already received the closed 50-digit tranche; any further promotion is a
separate scoped decision and merge.
