# C10a-05 frozen fixed-pacing protocol

Compare `control` (current mainline window finalization) with `collection`
(the same cadence/excess across shared SIQS windows, early zero rows and
charged terminal drain). No other mathematical, collector, storage, family,
multiplier, interval or solver field changes. There is one challenger; no
parameter sweep. This is not a repetition of the R3 cadence-32 experiment.

Before accepted timing, freeze runtime and all benchmark helper sources,
this protocol, source reconciliation manifest, complete configurations,
fixtures, seeds and starting mainline in `c10a05_training.json`. Commit the
freeze. Sources remain fixed through confirmation; any necessary correction
requires a new identity and disclosure, not replacement of prior evidence.
Research and feasibility captures remain separately labeled and excluded.

## Population and observations

Training uses the three previously revealed C10 confirmation inputs in each
40/50-digit band and the three C10 training inputs at 30 digits. Controls are
the accepted C9/C10 plan bundles at 40/50 and the retained C10 30-digit control.
Three small independent products of known primes use the reference SIQS
configuration (8-digit regression class). Cross each number with seeds
7, 29 and 47: nine matched samples, not nine independent integers.

The inspected baseline completed in 4.8/117.8 seconds at 40/50 digits; the
new policy also completed within the 240-second feasibility window. Together
with the historical complete-call receipts this supports 5/10/60/360-second
training calls for small/30/40/50 digits and 5/10/60/480-second confirmation
calls. These windows include headroom for fresh difficulty; neither 120 nor
1200 seconds is a universal ceiling or mandatory duration. A timeout remains
a censored result, not evidence of impossibility. Do not extend selected
individual timed samples post hoc. A later longer-window study would retain
these costs and define a new matched protocol.

Each call receives 10^13 work units, equal wall/CPU limits, the identical
per-band owned-memory/storage config (1 GiB for the growing 30/40/50 bundles),
and a 3 GiB process RSS watchdog. Deadlines are cooperative at bounded integer
operations. Full cost includes setup, failed downstream attempts, extra
collection, extraction, classification and independent reconstruction/certainty
validation. Unresolved calls cost the full assigned cap for selection; report
actual duration and charged work alongside it. A factor-one result whose
children are unresolved is not a complete factorization.

Every new process lease warms each active arm for at least three validated
seconds of both wall and CPU execution. Deterministic randomized order uses
seed `105005 + 100*digits + sample`. Cold process probes are separate bounded
calls; instrumented profiles and caller traces are excluded from accepted
timing evidence. Hold `/private/tmp/factor-performance.lock` for accepted
timings and heavy QA. No concurrent sessions require a separate handoff.

## Stability, selection and fresh confirmation

At nine samples extend to eighteen if paired signs differ within an input,
repeat-cell wall drift exceeds 15%, or first/last three-group mean paired
effects differ by over 15 percentage points. Keep every original sample;
instability after the final extension prevents a speed claim. These are
noise checks, not a minimum acceptable effect size. An incomplete extension
cannot pass confirmation even if its interim interval favors the candidate.

Rank training by validated completions, then sum of capped complete cost;
ties retain control. Advance only a challenger beating control in that order.
Freeze selection and raw-evidence hashes before generating fresh inputs.
Generate three new independent certified balanced products per selected band,
using seed 202610110505; exclude all n values in versioned corpora/controls
and the historical exclusion manifest. The recursive prime-certificate
generator conditions on a large factor of p−1; this is not an RSA-distributed
population. Previously inspected inputs remain training. Do not retune after
fresh values are revealed.

Confirmation repeats the nine/eighteen matched design and reports complete
counts, absolute complete/capped cost, medians, per-input effects, work,
additional positions/polynomials, stage totals and peak retained storage.
Hierarchical 4000-draw bootstrap (seed 105019) resamples independent inputs,
then matched samples. Adoption needs zero correctness failures, all planned
balanced samples complete in both arms, stable effects and a 95% complete-cost
change interval wholly below zero. No universal 10% floor. No more than a
five-percentage-point completion regression in another declared class; narrow
adoption to confirmed bands if other classes regress or remain inconclusive.
No additive C1/C9/C10/E1 speed claim.

## Resources and interruption

The tranche has 14,400 seconds each of aggregate charged wall/CPU work. Prepay
1800 seconds for all feasibility and heavy QA (including committed-only QA);
record actual costs against that reservation and stop for an explicit protocol
amendment if it is insufficient. Training has an additional 5400-second cap,
diagnostics 600, generation 120; confirmation shares the remaining aggregate
allowance. Captures <=256 MiB; diagnostic checkpoints <=16 MiB. Source reading
and human/model editing are not performance execution charges.

Reserve a whole missing matched group before starting. Persist an expense
reservation before **each arm**, then its inclusive wall/CPU charge; preserve
partial groups. Failed/interrupted calls keep at least their full reservation.
Hard-killed reserved calls remain charged on reload. Resume cannot reset
allowances or overwrite completed rows. Yield and stability markers have
unique lease timestamps, addressing the documented C10 repeated-lease failure
under this new runner identity without changing frozen C10 sources. Cold
request/partial evidence likewise must survive a failed subprocess.

Diagnostics include caller-boundary attribution, unsuccessful/first useful
dependencies, a 30-second profile per arm, and separately measured checkpoint
encoding, root/store rebuild and continuation under cumulative allowances.
Record stage and complete costs, proper factors and completion together;
fewer solve calls or extra relations alone cannot justify adoption.

Correctness gates: independent small arithmetic/matrix witnesses; early and
trivial dependencies; singleton retention; block, polynomial, finite search,
stalled/trivial and storage boundaries; interruption during preparation,
filtering, solving and extraction; work/wall/CPU/cancellation; repeated and
serialized resume; schemas 1–5 compatibility and schema 6 identity checks.
Run `make -C v2 test` and `make -C v2 lint`, then tests and all benchmark imports
from committed files only. Commit task-only changes, leave merging/pushing
separate, and close only the delivered C10a-05 scope after its gates pass.
