# C1 retained-data cost-attribution audit

This is an explicit post-observation diagnostic repair, not a replacement for
any frozen verdict or fresh performance confirmation. Freeze this audit before
running it. Preserve the original screen, follow-up, source hashes and captures.
No additional inputs, positions, residual bounds, split attempts or collection
runs are authorized by this audit.

The original follow-up charges each candidate for both competing policies,
SLP analysis, repeated searches and every square-root extraction after a
complete factorization. Its exhaustive-validation result remains valid, but
that charge is not the cost of completing one candidate factorization. The
first 40-digit input has a verified DLP factor at 2,087 blocks with no SLP
factor, yet this witness fails only after charging subsequent/other-arm work.
At the first 50-digit input, exhaustive DLP extraction hits the local analysis
deadline. These observations motivate a cost-attribution repair; they do not
establish a production speedup or authorize weakening arithmetic validation.

Use only the six already fixed follow-up populations, after collection ends.
Keep the original endpoint, product, certification, record and memory limits.
Analyze policy 128 (the pre-existing first refinement policy) and SLP. For
an existing verified DLP-before-SLP witness, inspect the earliest recorded
such prefix. Otherwise inspect retained block midpoints between the last
uncensored incomplete DLP checkpoint and the first censored or complete DLP
checkpoint; at most eight midpoints. An exhausted record/collection limit
excludes that terminal prefix, not complete strictly earlier prefixes.
Stop each factor extraction after its first independently verified complete
factorization. Verify all generated kernel masks algebraically against the
original rows; independently reconstruct exact exponents, square corrections
and both GCDs for every extracted dependency. Report generated algebraic masks
and extracted/verified square congruences separately, never labeling an
unextracted mask an independently checked square congruence.

For each directly verified witness charge the enclosing original checkpoint's
entire policy-128 split/certification CPU (a conservative upper bound), plus
all graph, provenance, filtering, matrix, validation and extraction CPU for
that witness. Report separate SLP/candidate costs and the entire diagnostic
search/validation overhead. Keep the original 50%-of-control allowance, the
16 additional LP constraints, two independent inputs in one band, and at least
one same-position SLP shortfall. This exploratory attribution result can
justify implementation investment only; it cannot promote defaults or claim
a factoring speedup. The original literal gate outcome is always reported
beside it. Production must still pass new frozen matched-resource training
and fresh confirmation, including actual bounded ownership/eviction costs.

The audit has at most 30 wall/CPU seconds and 10^12 work per cell, 180 seconds
total, and must fit within the unspent part of the existing 4,500-second
follow-up study allowance (including the earlier retained-record analysis).
At most 16 MiB new capture output within the existing 512 MiB study cap.
Hold the same machine-wide lock. No further collection or audit expansion
follows a failure or inconclusive outcome. Preserve failures and whole-input
unresolved results, with explicit censoring. This correction is post hoc and
must be disclosed as such in the final investment decision.
