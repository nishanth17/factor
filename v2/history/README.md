Git history preserves original prose and quantitative records. No duplicate
full roadmap is maintained. Frozen protocols, source snapshots and verdicts
retain their historical identities; current guides link to the applicable
package or concise results instead of repeating the complete narrative.

Detailed reports and generated results are maintained locally, rather than as
a selective public studies collection. The former collection remains in Git
history; its removal from the current tree does not erase earlier commits.
Frozen source manifests keep their original paths and hashes. Historical
campaigns require their pinned source revision, rather than current mainline.
